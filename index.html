The band

For more than 25 years, the Melvin Beddow Big Band has brought the thrill of a full live band to theatres, dances and celebrations across Essex.

With two vocalists and a wide repertoire, every performance is shaped around the room — from elegant swing standards to jive, jazz, rock & roll and crowd-pleasing pop.
