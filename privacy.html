Information we collect

When you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide. This may include:

  • Your name, email address and telephone number.
  • Your proposed event date, venue and type of event.
  • Your messages and our correspondence with you.
  • Booking and payment records where you arrange a performance.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information or details about other people unless these are needed for your enquiry.

Our hosting and website service providers may also process technical information, such as your IP address, browser details and the time of a request, when delivering and protecting the website.

How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and discuss availability.
  • Provide quotations and arrange performances.
  • Communicate with you about your booking.
  • Maintain necessary business and financial records.
  • Operate, maintain and protect our website.

Our reasons for using your information

For enquiries about a booking you intend to make yourself, and for managing that booking, we use information to take steps at your request before entering into a contract or to fulfil that contract.

For general correspondence, enquiries made on behalf of an organisation and website security, we rely on our legitimate interests in running the band, responding to people who contact us and keeping our services secure.

Where we must retain information to meet a legal requirement, such as applicable accounting obligations, we rely on that legal obligation.

Testimonials

We publish testimonials with the contributor's permission. These may include their name, comments, event type and event date. You can withdraw that permission by contacting us, and we will remove the testimonial from our website.

Published testimonials are publicly accessible and may be indexed by search engines or copied by others.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and our reason for using your information, you may have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of your personal information.
  • Have inaccurate or incomplete information corrected.
  • Request deletion of your information.
  • Ask us to restrict how we use your information.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Receive certain information in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on it, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

To make a request, email enquiries@mbbb.co.uk. We may need to confirm your identity before responding.
